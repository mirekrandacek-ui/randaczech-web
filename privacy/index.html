Privacy Policy

RandaCzech Studio · Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy covers the mobile apps published by RandaCzech Studio: Block Crumble: Puzzle Game, DartScore Pro and Země Město (Stop: Categories Word Game). It explains what data the apps use and why.

What we do not do

  • You do not need to create an account in our apps.
  • We do not run our own servers that collect your personal data.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, settings, scores and unlocked items are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Advertising

Our apps are free and show ads served by Google AdMob and its advertising partners. To show ads, these providers may collect and process the advertising ID of your device, IP address, device and app information and ad interaction data. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). See Google's advertising policy and Google's privacy policy.

Purchases

Where an app offers a purchase (for example removing ads), it is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Google Play Games (optional)

Some apps let you sign in with Google Play Games to use leaderboards. Signing in is optional. If you sign in, Google provides your player name and score to the app and processes this data under Google's privacy policy.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page, with its date at the top.
